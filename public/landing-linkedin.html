FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Torque Network?

Torque Network is a LinkedIn growth community for early-career professionals. Members share the posts they publish, peers from the same industry engage with the ones they find useful, and everyone gets weekly topic ideas and templates to keep posting consistently.

Who can join?

Freshers and professionals with roughly 6 months to 8 years of experience who want to post on LinkedIn consistently and grow a personal brand in their industry.

How much time does it take each week?

You can share up to 2 of your own posts a week, and you choose which peer posts to engage with — around 20 a week. Most members fit it into a few short sessions.

Is it safe for my LinkedIn account?

Yes. All engagement is written by real professionals by hand. There are no bots, scripts or auto-comment tools, and nobody is assigned posts — you only engage with what genuinely interests you.

How much does it cost?

Torque for LinkedIn is ₹500 per month, billed monthly. You can cancel anytime from your account.
